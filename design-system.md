# DESIGN SYSTEM — Visual Quality Guidelines

A website generated by the AI Local Business Website Factory must look like something a professional web agency could deliver — not like an obvious AI template.

---

## What we are NOT doing

- Excessive gradients on every section.
- Random glassmorphism for no reason.
- Meaningless animations (parallax, scroll-jacking, etc.).
- Huge generic headings ("Welcome to Our World").
- AI-generated illustrations everywhere.
- Cluttered cards with overlapping shadows.
- Excessive rounded corners as a style.
- Template-feel layouts that look the same across every business.

---

## What we ARE doing

- Strong typography.
- Generous spacing.
- Clear visual hierarchy.
- Real photography (user-uploaded photos of the business, food, team, location, etc.).
- Readable contrast.
- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Conversion clarity (clear CTA, phone, WhatsApp).
- Business-specific visual identity (color, type, voice).

---

## Typography

- Use a small set of reliable fonts (e.g. one serif + one sans-serif, or a strong sans-serif pair).
- Strong hierarchy: clear H1, H2, H3, body, caption sizes.
- Line length optimized for readability (45-75 characters per line).
- Line height generous (1.5-1.7 for body text).

**Font recommendations (renderer can pick from a curated set)**:
- Modern: Inter / DM Sans / Manrope
- Classic: Playfair Display + Lato
- Bold: Space Grotesk
- Friendly: Nunito / Quicksand
- Editorial: Fraunces + Source Sans

---

## Color

- Maximum 2-3 brand colors per site.
- 1 primary, 1 accent, 1 neutral background.
- Avoid neon and overly saturated palettes.
- Test contrast for accessibility (WCAG AA at minimum).

**Suggested palette profiles** (renderer can use these as defaults):
- Warm (Restaurant): Deep red, cream, charcoal.
- Spa (Salon): Sage green, off-white, soft gold.
- Academic (Coaching): Navy, off-white, warm gold.
- Custom: User-selected via brand input.

---

## Spacing

- Consistent vertical rhythm.
- Generous section padding (vertical breathing room).
- Avoid cramped layouts.
- Mobile padding smaller but still comfortable.

---

## Photography

- Real user-uploaded photos are the visual backbone.
- Hero images: full-bleed, optimized for performance.
- Gallery: real business photos (food, salon, classroom, team).
- Avoid AI-generated stock-looking images.
- Image alt text always generated.

---

## Components (section quality rules)

**Hero**:
- One strong headline.
- Sub-headline for context.
- One primary CTA.
- Optional secondary CTA.
- High-quality photo background with overlay for readability.
- NOT a generic AI illustration.

**About**:
- Real photo of business/team/space.
- 2-4 paragraphs max.
- Conversational tone.

**Services**:
- Clear name + short description.
- Price if provided.
- One image per service (or none, gracefully).
- Layout: cards or list based on count.

**Gallery**:
- 3-12 real photos.
- Captions optional but useful.
- Layout: grid or masonry.

**Testimonials**:
- 2-6 real quotes (or none — if none, section hidden).
- Author + role.
- Honest. No fake reviews.

**Team**:
- Optional.
- Photo + name + role + 1-line bio.

**FAQ**:
- 3-8 real questions (or none).
- Concise answers.

**Booking / Contact**:
- Clear call/WhatsApp button.
- Optional form.
- Hours if available.
- Address with map link.

**Location**:
- Map embed.
- Address.
- Optional directions/landmarks.

**CTA banner**:
- Strong single message.
- One button.

**Footer**:
- Business name.
- Contact summary.
- Social links.
- Copyright.

---

## Mobile

- Mobile-first.
- Tap targets minimum 44x44 px.
- Phone/WhatsApp buttons always easy to reach.
- Form fields mobile-friendly.
- No horizontal scroll.

---

## Accessibility

- WCAG AA contrast minimum.
- Alt text for all images.
- Semantic HTML (header, main, nav, section, footer).
- Keyboard navigable.
- Form labels.

---

## Performance

- Optimized images (WebP preferred).
- Minimal JavaScript.
- Inline critical CSS.
- Lazy load below-the-fold images.
- Fast time-to-interactive.

---

## What "professional" means here

- Looks like a real agency-built site for a real business.
- Information is easy to find.
- Reading flow makes sense.
- CTAs are obvious.
- Mobile experience is excellent.
- Business feels represented accurately, not generically.

# COMPETITIVE RESEARCH — Landscape and Gap Analysis

This document surveys the current landscape of AI website builders and local website services, identifies the gap, and explains our differentiation.

---

## The landscape

### AI Website Builders (Global)

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness |
|------------|----------|----------|
| Wix ADI | Fast, drag-and-drop, decent templates | Generic output, complex builder |
| Bookmark | AI-driven layout suggestions | Not business-category-aware |
| Durable | Fast AI generation, simple UX | Quality varies, not India-focused |
| Framer | Beautiful design, high-end output | Overkill for local businesses, expensive |
| Squarespace | Professional templates, good design | Requires manual setup, not AI-native |
| Hostinger AI Builder | Cheap, fast | Low quality, obviously templated |
| 10Web AI | WordPress-based, decent quality | Complex, WordPress dependency |
| Duda | Agency-focused, good templates | Not AI-first, expensive |

**Pattern**: Most AI builders are general-purpose. They accept prompts and produce generic-looking pages. The business data is secondary — the AI does its best with whatever the user provides in text form.

### Local Website Services (India)

| Service | What it is | Gap |
|---------|-----------|-----|
| Local agencies | Full-service ₹15,000-₹50,000+ | Expensive, slow, requires hand-holding |
| WIX / Squarespace resellers | DIY with agency help | Still requires technical understanding |
| Website builders (LocalAdvantage, etc.) | Template-based with Indian categories | Often outdated UX, not AI-native |
| Free DIY tools (Google Sites, Canva) | Free but ugly | Not professional enough for businesses |
| Job board listings for freelancers | ₹3,000-₹10,000 | Quality inconsistent, slow |

**Pattern**: At the ₹5,000-₹15,000 range in India, there's a gap: too expensive for free DIY tools, but too cheap for full agency work. Quality is inconsistent.

## The gap

### What competitors do well

- Fast generation (minutes, not days).
- Low/no code required.
- Mobile responsive.
- Basic SEO.

### What competitors do poorly

1. **Generic output**: Most AI builders produce websites that look like AI websites. The same card layouts, the same heading styles, the same AI-generated illustrations.
2. **No business model awareness**: A restaurant and a dentist get similar-looking websites with different text. The sections don't match the business type.
3. **Fabrication**: AI invents facts, opening hours, prices, reviews. Dangerous for real businesses.
4. **No structured data collection**: Free-form prompt input doesn't capture what matters (services, prices, hours, photos).
5. **No review gate**: AI auto-generates and publishes. Business owner has limited control.
6. **Not India-first**: Phone formats, UPI, WhatsApp, INR, local SEO, Google Maps — these are second-class or missing.
7. **No conversion optimization**: Call button, WhatsApp deep link, enquiry form — these are inconsistently implemented.
8. **No ongoing relationship**: After the site is built, there's no maintenance, no updates, no reason to stay.

## Our differentiation

### 1. Business-data-first, not prompt-first

Most builders: User types a prompt → AI guesses → generic site.

Our system: User fills a structured form → AI knows the business → appropriate site.

This means:
- Restaurant → Menu section.
- Salon → Services + Pricing.
- Coaching → Courses + Results.

The AI doesn't have to guess. The structure tells it what sections to use.

### 2. Professional section system

We compose websites from a curated section library. Sections are business-type-aware and render consistently.

No random layouts. No AI inventing a new section type on the fly.

### 3. Honesty as a feature

We will not fabricate business facts. Unknown = ask or omit.

This is a trust signal that no competitor is explicitly making.

### 4. India-first conversion

- WhatsApp deep links (direct chat without saving number).
- UPI payment links (future).
- Phone/click-to-call as primary CTA.
- Google Maps embed as standard.
- INR pricing display.
- Indian phone format validation.
- Hindi content possible (future).

### 5. Review gate

Every site is reviewed before publish. Business owner sees exactly what will be published.

### 6. Maintenance relationship

₹499/month isn't just hosting. It's "your site stays current, you can make edits, you have support."

This creates recurring revenue and a relationship, not a one-off transaction.

### 7. Quality bar

We won't ship a site we'd be embarrassed to show a paying customer.

"Would we show this to a real business?" is the bar.

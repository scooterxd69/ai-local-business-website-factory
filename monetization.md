# MONETIZATION — How the Product Makes Money

This document evaluates monetization options for the AI Local Business Website Factory and recommends an initial strategy.

---

## Constraints

- The product must eventually be sellable to real businesses.
- Local Indian SMBs have modest budgets.
- The product should produce recurring revenue, not just one-off income.
- Pricing must be simple enough to explain in one sentence.

---

## Option A — One-time setup fee

**Range**: ₹2,000 – ₹15,000 per site.

**Pros**:
- Simple to understand.
- Easy to sell.
- No subscription friction.

**Cons**:
- No recurring revenue.
- Customer leaves after one project.
- Limited incentive to maintain quality.
- Hard to fund ongoing development.

**Verdict**: Good for early traction; insufficient alone.

---

## Option B — Monthly subscription

**Range**: ₹300 – ₹2,000/month.

**Pros**:
- Recurring revenue.
- Predictable for the business.
- Covers hosting, maintenance, updates.

**Cons**:
- Local businesses are subscription-averse.
- Hard to differentiate "what am I paying for each month?".
- Churn risk.

**Verdict**: Possible, but needs a clear value story.

---

## Option C — Hybrid (setup + subscription)

**Range**: ₹3,000 – ₹8,000 setup + ₹300 – ₹800/month maintenance.

**Pros**:
- Recurring revenue with a paid initial commitment.
- Subscription justified by hosting, updates, analytics, content refreshes.
- Aligns incentives: product must keep providing value.
- Familiar pattern in Indian SMB market (CA, CRM, etc.).

**Cons**:
- More complex to explain.
- Risk of churn at the renewal point.

**Verdict**: Recommended. This is the dominant model in Indian SMB SaaS.

---

## Option D — Agency model

The product is sold or licensed to web agencies / freelancers, who use it to build sites for their clients at scale.

**Pros**:
- Higher revenue per site.
- Agencies sell the customer relationship.
- Scales without direct sales.

**Cons**:
- Requires feature set agencies care about (multi-tenant, white-label, client management).
- Slower feedback loop.
- Bigger product surface.

**Verdict**: Strong second act. Build a usable single-tenant MVP first, then offer agency mode.

---

## Option E — Marketplace / template store

Sell premium templates or themes through a marketplace.

**Verdict**: Premature. Focus on getting the engine right first.

---

## Recommended initial strategy

**Hybrid model (Option C)** with clear subscription value:

- **Setup fee**: ₹4,999 – ₹7,999 depending on category complexity.
- **Monthly maintenance**: ₹499/month, includes:
  - Hosting.
  - SSL.
  - Basic analytics.
  - Minor content edits.
  - Optional AI-driven content refreshes (future).

**Why this works in India**:
- Setup fee is below typical "agency website" cost (₹15,000-₹50,000) but well above "free AI website" perceived value.
- Monthly ₹499 is comparable to common SMB SaaS (domain + email + minor tools).
- Subscription is small enough to feel like "hosting + small upkeep", not a big commitment.
- Bundle value: "Your website is hosted, secure, and editable. ₹499/month covers it."

**Pricing principles**:
- Display price inclusive of taxes.
- INR pricing by default.
- Annual discount (e.g. ₹5,000/year instead of ₹5,988).
- Free preview of the site before payment.
- Optional payment via UPI / cards / netbanking.

---

## Upsell paths (future)

- Custom domain (₹800/year + setup).
- Additional categories.
- Additional languages.
- AI-driven monthly content refresh.
- Extra pages (separate landing pages for campaigns).
- Photography / asset package.

---

## Agency mode (future, post-MVP)

After MVP is stable:
- Multi-tenant support.
- White-label option.
- Bulk client management.
- Volume pricing.
- Custom branding on generated sites.

This becomes a B2B revenue stream on top of B2C.

---

## What we are NOT doing for MVP

- No billing system for MVP.
- No payments integration.
- No agency features.
- No custom domain.

The MVP proves the product. Monetization experiments come after.

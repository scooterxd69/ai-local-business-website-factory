# VISION — AI Local Business Website Factory

## What we are building

A system that takes structured information about a real local business and produces a professional, mobile-responsive website in minutes — without requiring the customer to write prompts or understand AI.

The long-term goal is a product that can be sold to or used to serve **actual paying local businesses**.

---

## The problem

Small local businesses (dentists, salons, restaurants, coaches, repair shops, tutors) need a website but:

- Don't know how to design one.
- Don't want to learn a website builder.
- Don't trust generic AI website generators that produce obviously templated, low-quality results.
- Don't have the time to write prompts, upload assets, and configure sections.
- Have a budget that doesn't justify a ₹50,000+ agency site.
- Need something that looks professional and converts enquiries, not a "AI-made" badge of shame.

---

## Our thesis

A **structured, business-aware** approach produces dramatically better results than a free-form "prompt → website" model.

If we model local businesses as structured data (identity, services, location, hours, brand, social), and we model websites as composed sections (Hero, Services, About, Location, etc.) selected by an AI based on business category, we get:

- Predictable, editable output.
- Reusable, professional design.
- Honest content (no fabrication).
- A system that a non-technical person can use.

---

## What success looks like

**A real local business** in India (e.g. Sharma Dental Clinic in Prayagraj) provides:

- Name
- Category
- Location
- 4 services
- Phone
- Hours
- 5 photos
- Instagram handle

…and within minutes has a beautiful, mobile-first website they can preview, edit, approve, and publish to a public URL.

The website would be something we would be comfortable handing to a paying customer.

---

## What we are NOT building (yet)

- A general-purpose AI website builder that accepts any prompt.
- A complex drag-and-drop CMS.
- A multi-tenant SaaS with billing, agencies, white-label, etc.
- An enterprise platform with SSO, audit logs, compliance.
- A marketplace for templates.
- A voice agent or chatbot infrastructure.

These can come later. MVP proves the core thesis.

---

## Long-term product direction

The MVP proves the engine. The product strategy evolves in phases:

1. **MVP** — Onboard 3 categories (Restaurant, Salon, Coaching). Self-serve onboarding. AI generation. Review/edit. Publish. Subdomain hosting.
2. **Agency mode** — Let local freelancers/agencies use the factory to build sites for their clients at scale.
3. **Maintenance subscription** — Monthly fee for updates, content refreshes, and hosting.
4. **India expansion** — UPI, WhatsApp deep links, INR pricing, multilingual content (Hindi/English), Google Business Profile sync.
5. **Internationalization** — Architecture supports i18n from day 1; later enable more regions.
6. **Custom domains & analytics** — Add when retention is proven.
7. **AI maintenance** — Periodic AI-driven content refreshes, SEO suggestions, post updates.

---

## Why this is defensible

- **Local-business-first schema** — most AI builders are template-first; we are data-first.
- **Quality bar** — section system + design system + review flow beats "AI made a page".
- **WhatsApp/India first** — local conversion mechanics matter.
- **Honesty rule** — no fabrication is a trust signal competitors ignore.
- **Repeatable** — once a category is modeled, scaling is cheap.

# MVP SCOPE — What the First Version Proves

---

## The MVP thesis

> A real local business can enter structured information and receive a professional website they are comfortable showing to a customer.

That is the bar. Not more.

---

## MVP categories (3 only)

1. **Restaurant** — menu, hero, gallery, reviews, location, contact.
2. **Salon** — services, pricing, gallery, testimonials, booking/contact.
3. **Coaching Institute** — courses, results, faculty/testimonials, FAQ, contact.

These cover diverse section types: menus, pricing, booking, testimonials, courses, staff, FAQs. They stress-test the section system.

---

## What the MVP includes

### Business onboarding (structured form)
- Category selection.
- Identity: name, tagline, description.
- Services / Products list (structured).
- Contact: phone, email, WhatsApp.
- Location: address, city, state, country, Google Maps info.
- Hours.
- Brand/style preferences.
- Photos upload.
- Social links.

### AI generation pipeline
- Structured prompt template per category.
- Structured JSON output matching `website-schema.md`.
- Theme proposal (colors, fonts).
- Copy generation for all sections.
- SEO title + description.
- Alt text for images.

### Website rendering
- Clean HTML + responsive CSS.
- Section assembly based on category profile.
- Photography-focused design (not AI illustrations).
- Mobile-first.
- Professional typography and spacing.

### Review and editor
- View generated site.
- Edit any text.
- Toggle sections on/off.
- Change theme parameters.
- Replace images.
- Approve.

### Publishing
- Preview URL.
- Published URL (subdomain for MVP).
- Basic analytics.

---

## What the MVP excludes

- More than 3 business categories.
- Drag-and-drop builder.
- Complex CMS.
- Enterprise authentication.
- Custom domain (later).
- Multilingual content (architecture supports; not required for MVP).
- Agency mode.
- Agency white-label.
- Payments / billing.
- AI maintenance / content refreshes.
- Complex SEO optimization.
- Social media integrations beyond basic link.

---

## Quality bar for MVP success

Before declaring MVP ready, evaluate with real sample businesses:

> Would we be comfortable showing this website to a paying business?

Evaluate on:
- Visual quality (professional, not obviously AI-made).
- Factual accuracy (no fabricated business claims).
- Mobile usability.
- Performance (loads quickly).
- Readability (clear copy, hierarchy).
- Conversion (clear CTA, contact info, WhatsApp link).
- SEO basics (title, meta, structured data).

If the answer is "no" for any of these, fix it before declaring success.
